PROCEDURE -- work through these steps in order before you answer.

Step 1. Decide whether this is a job ad at all. Look for an open position, duties and
        requirements. Notes, articles and company blurbs are not job ads. If it is not one,
        set is_job_ad false and stop.
Step 2. Read the responsibilities section first, before the title. The responsibilities say
        what the job actually is; the title is marketing. Choose `role` from them.
Step 3. Find the seniority signal, in this order: an explicit years-of-experience line, then
        the scope language (owning a roadmap, mentoring, setting direction), then the title.
        Use the first one you find.
Step 4. Walk the ad top to bottom and list every skill, tool and technology mentioned.
        Do not judge them yet.
Step 5. For each item, find the sentence that mentions it and copy the shortest phrase (at
        most 15 words) that proves it. If no such phrase exists, delete the item.
Step 6. Now assign criticality from the wording of that quote. Hedged wording
        ("familiarity", "a plus", "preferred") goes down a level. Repeated across sections
        goes to critical.
Step 7. Merge duplicates and aliases into one entry each.
Step 8. Re-read the title and first paragraph, and count what appears two or more times
        anywhere. That is `emphasis`, ordered by prominence.
Step 9. Check every `evidence` value is a real quote from the ad. Delete any that is not.

PROCEDURE -- work through these steps in order before you answer.

Step 1. Read the requirements list. Find the specific requirement this question tests. If
        you cannot point to one, `grounded` is false.
Step 2. Count the asks. Look for "and", "also", "as well as", and question marks. More than
        one thing being asked means `single_ask` is false, even when the halves are related.
Step 3. Ask yourself: would an engineer doing this job actually say this sentence out loud in
        an interview? Quiz phrasing, definitions with no application, and puzzle questions
        make `realistic` false.
Step 4. Ask whether the answer is already printed in the job ad, or findable in one search.
        Either makes `realistic` false.
Step 5. Judge difficulty against the seniority AND the difficulty setting together. A
        definition question at senior/hard is too_easy. A distributed-architecture question
        at junior/easy is too_hard. Only mark about_right when it fits both.
Step 6. Write `comment` as one sentence naming the biggest single problem, or what makes it
        good if you found no problem.

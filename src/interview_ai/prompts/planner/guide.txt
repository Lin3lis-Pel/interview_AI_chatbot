PROCEDURE -- work through these steps in order before you answer.

Step 1. Group the requirements into clusters that a single interviewer would probe in one
        stretch of conversation. Aim for 3-6 technical clusters.
Step 2. Name each cluster the way practitioners in this role name it. Reject any name you
        could paste onto a different job.
Step 3. Add exactly one behavioural/communication subject.
Step 4. Rank the technical subjects: count the critical requirements in each, then break
        ties with the company emphasis list.
Step 5. Give the behavioural subject a weight between 5 and 15. Distribute the remainder
        across the technical subjects in the ranked order from Step 4, largest first.
Step 6. Shift weight according to seniority: for junior, move some weight from architecture
        toward fundamentals and project work; for senior or lead, move it the other way.
Step 7. Add up the weights. If the total is not exactly 100, adjust the largest subject by
        the difference and add them up again.
Step 8. For each subject, write `grounded_in` by copying the requirement names it covers.
        Any subject with an empty list must be deleted, and its weight redistributed.
Step 9. Write each `rationale` as one sentence naming what in the ad bought that weight.

PROCEDURE -- work through these steps in order before you answer.

Step 1. Group the transcript turns by subject and average the five scores within each
        subject. That average, on the 1-5 scale, is the `per_topic` score.
Step 2. Convert to 0-100: multiply each subject average by that subject weight, sum, and
        scale from the 1-5 range. Sanity-check the result against the transcript -- an
        interview of vague answers cannot come out above 50.
Step 3. Walk the critical and important requirements one by one. For each, find the turn
        that tested it. If no turn tested it, or the turn scored below 3, it belongs in
        `unmet_requirements`.
Step 4. Pick the two or three highest-scoring subjects for `strengths`, and quote what the
        candidate actually did well rather than praising them in general.
Step 5. Pick the lowest-scoring subjects for `weaknesses`, and name the specific gap from the
        `missing` lists in the transcript.
Step 6. List the questions whose scores were lowest in `struggled_questions`.
Step 7. Turn each unmet requirement into one concrete, checkable study action. Order them by
        how much they would move a real interview per hour spent.
Step 8. Choose the recommendation by asking only this: did they demonstrate the critical
        requirements? Failing a critical requirement caps the answer at borderline.

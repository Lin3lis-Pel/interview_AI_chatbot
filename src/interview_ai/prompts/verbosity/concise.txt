RESPONSE LENGTH: concise
Every piece of text the candidate reads has a hard word budget.
- Each interview question: ONE sentence, at most 25 words. No scene-setting, no list of
  sub-questions -- ask for the single most revealing thing.
- `recommendation_reason`: at most 2 sentences.
- Every per-topic comment, strength, weakness and study-plan item: at most 15 words, naming
  the specific gap or action and nothing else.

This changes LENGTH ONLY. It must not change what is asked, what is scored, or the scoring
bar -- a short question still targets the same requirement, and a short report still states
every unmet requirement.
